About the Conference

MIC brings together student researchers for an afternoon of chemistry-focused poster presentations and open discussion. The conference is organized by Minjok Journal, operating as an academic society registered with Korea's National Research Foundation (NRF), and is hosted at Minjok Leadership Academy. It runs as a companion "side quest" alongside the International Chemistry Tournament (IChTO) — widening the event to researchers and competitors from across the field of chemistry.

Excellent Poster Presentation Award

Roughly the top 10% of presenters will be recognized with an award presented under the name of the Headmaster of Minjok Leadership Academy.

What to prepare and how presentations will run on the day.

Topic

Free / open. Presentations may cover any chemistry-related research, experiment, or project.

Format

Poster presentation followed by an open, free-form question-and-answer session with attendees and judges.

Guidelines

Detailed poster formatting guidelines will be announced to confirmed presenters after registration closes.

Presenter Booths

Tap a booth to preview the poster and abstract. More booths will be added as presenters apply.

Preparing to Present

  • Bring a printed poster summarizing your research question, methods, and findings.
  • Be ready to give a short informal walkthrough of your poster to visiting attendees.
  • Prepare for open, unscripted questions from both fellow attendees and judges.
  • Presentations and Q&A are conducted in English.
